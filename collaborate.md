# Consulting

[Dianoetic](https://www.dianoetic.com.au) is my new, part-time consultancy for research, policy analysis and nonprofit data work. Availability is limited.

I have 12 years of research experience and have worked in nonprofit data roles since 2018, mostly as an employee, with some short project roles. I know the constraints small teams work under: limited budget, mixed technical skills, and funder reporting that has to be right.

## Experience in

- Data strategy and architecture
- CRM implementation and integration (Salesforce, NationBuilder, Zapier)
- Data pipelines and automation (Python, SQL, Power Automate)
- Reporting and dashboards (Power BI, Tableau)
- Economic research and modelling
- Training, documentation and change management

## How I work

- **Sized to the problem.** A small team that needs a contact database does not need a custom build.
- **Handover.** Documentation and training, so your team can run the system without me.
- **Past results.** A Salesforce CRM built for a senior management team, saving over $50,000 against the projected development cost, and automation that reclaimed more than 500 staff hours a year at a nonprofit.
- **Offline-first option.** For organisations that can't or won't use the cloud, see the Offline CRM demo in my [portfolio](/portfolio/).
- **Pro bono.** I take on a small number of pro bono projects for small nonprofits. See [Dianoetic](https://www.dianoetic.com.au/services/).
